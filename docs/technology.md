# Specialized Technology

## Chosen technology
Learning analytics using Classical Test Theory (CTT) item analysis, with data visualization .

## Why I chose it
My problem is that lecturers cannot easily tell which multiple-choice questions are too easy, too hard, or unable to separate strong students from weak ones. CTT item analysis answers this with two measures calculated from students' responses:
- Difficulty Index (P): the proportion of students who answered the question correctly.
- Discrimination Index (D): how well the question separates the upper and lower groups of students (normally the top and bottom 27%).

A dashboard then shows these results as charts and flags the questions that need review. CTT is simple for lecturers to understand and check, and it works with the small class sizes of a single course.

## Alternative considered
Item Response Theory (IRT) is more advanced, but it usually needs larger samples and specialised software, and its results are harder for lecturers to read. I chose CTT for this project.
